CEI provides four core services: Global Sourcing, Contract Manufacturing, Import/Export Logistics (C-TPAT certified), and Warehousing & Distribution (120,000 ft² facility in Batavia, IL). We manage every link in your supply chain from concept to final delivery.
C-TPAT certification means your shipments meet U.S. Customs and Border Protection's highest security standards, allowing faster customs clearance with reduced inspections, fewer delays, and greater cost efficiency for your supply chain.
CEI has developed long-term alliances with manufacturers primarily in Asia (including China) and Europe. Our entire factory network consists exclusively of ISO, QS and TS certified suppliers — ensuring consistent quality regardless of origin.
Our Batavia, IL facility offers 120,000 ft² of temperature-controlled storage with 27-foot ceilings, 9 bay stations, motion-sensor lighting, high-capacity sprinklers, and 24/7 security. We provide flexible, client-specific storage solutions to lower your inventory costs.
